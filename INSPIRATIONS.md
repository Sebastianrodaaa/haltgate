# Inspirations

Papers and projects that shaped haltgate. The arXiv IDs for the 2026 papers are as cited in the project brief
(docs/brief/SCOUT_BRIEF.md); they were checked there, not re-verified here.

- How to Loop Your Transformer, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40325 — which blocks to loop, how often, how state passes.
- Spherical Fixed-Point Reasoners, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25646 — geometric adaptive loop halting.
- Truncate or Not?, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26030 — truncated BPTT for looped transformers.
- One Model, Two Paths, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33517 — single-pass vs looped performance.
- Decision Models as Policy Gates, https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02155 — typed allow/deny/route classifiers with error rates.
- Angelopoulos, Bates, Candès, Jordan, Lei. Learn then Test: Calibrating Predictive Algorithms to Achieve Risk Control (2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01052
- Schuster et al. Confident Adaptive Language Modeling (CALM, 2022), https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07061
- Banino, Balaguer, Blundell. PonderNet (2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.05407
- Graves. Adaptive Computation Time (2016), https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08983
- Dehghani et al. Universal Transformers (2018), https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03819
- Raposo et al. Mixture-of-Depths (2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02258
- Gustave Doré and Albrecht Dürer, for the gate and the hourglass on the banner.
- athanor (https://github.com/ghgoodreau/athanor), for the README shape.
