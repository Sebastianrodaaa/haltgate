## Images in the banner (docs/screenshots/banner.png, sources in docs/assets/)

- Gustave Doré, *Inferno, Canto III: "Abandon all hope, ye who enter here"* (1861 engraving, plate 8 of Dante's Divine Comedy).
  Public domain. Source: Wikimedia Commons,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustave_Doré_-_Dante_Alighieri_-_Inferno_-_Plate_8_(Canto_III_-_Abandon_all_hope_ye_who_enter_here).jpg
- Albrecht Dürer, *Melencolia I* (1514 engraving). Public domain. Source: Wikimedia Commons,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dürer_Melancholia_I.jpg

Both were cropped, faded and dithered to two tones (Floyd-Steinberg) by `scripts/banner.py`.
